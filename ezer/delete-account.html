Delete your EZER account

EZER  ·  7308 Forest Bend Drive, Parker, Texas  ·  daniel4chigs@gmail.com

In the app

  1. Open EZER and go to Settings › Account.
  2. Tap Delete your account, then Delete everything.

Deletion is immediate. Every bank connection is revoked with Plaid first, then your account is erased.

Without the app

Email daniel4chigs@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my EZER account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, sign-in details) and any connected sign-in providers.
  • Bank connections, transactions, balances and detected subscriptions.
  • Savings goals, card designs and early-access list entries.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Savings transfer and ledger records: 7 years, as financial records, separated from your identity.
  • A record of your consent (date and policy version): 3 years.
  • Encrypted backups and logs: rotated out within 30 days.

Details are in our privacy policy and data retention policy.
