Impact Threads · Drop V·I

Skate graphic tees, drawn like board graphics

The best skate graphics were never just decoration. They were drawings that said something, usually something the adults did not like, printed on the bottom of a board or the back of a tee. Impact Threads is built on that tradition: illustrated back prints about what is happening right now.

The drawings

Beware Killer Drones looks like a page from a sketchbook: kids in a field, one flying a drone on a string like a kite, "B.W.D." lettered over the top and "beware killer drones" scrawled underneath. Ice x NYC is drawn in the same sepia, with masked agents in body armour standing over someone on the ground and smoke rising behind them. Bibi x NYC goes full colour, a giant Netanyahu in a suit stepping over a broken city while people scatter. Niger Delta Oil Pirates is the most painted of the four: masked gunmen packed into a boat on open water.

Built to be worn, not framed

Each print sits on a unisex softstyle tee with a small IMPACT script on the chest, so you can skate in it, go to class in it, and the front stays low-key. The art is on the back where people behind you see it, the same way a board graphic shows when you flip it.

Colours and sizes

White or black in S, M and L, except Niger Delta Oil Pirates, which comes in black. Size up if you want it to hang loose. The full size chart is in the size guide on the main site.

About the brand

Impact Threads is a small label for people who grew up on skate videos, rap and memes about the news. One drop at a time, four prints in the current one. $28.99 each, printed when you order. Two or more ship free in the US, and checkout runs through Shopify.

If a board graphic ever made you look something up, that is the feeling these are going for.
