Graphic tees with meaning

A graphic tee with meaning is not the same as a tee with words on it. Words tell people what you think. A picture with meaning makes them ask. Every Impact Threads print is built that way: one illustrated scene on the back, one short tagline on the site, and the rest is up to whoever is looking.

No. I, Beware Killer Drones

Kids in a field are flying a drone on a string, the way kids used to fly kites. The joke is that it is not a joke. Drones have become ordinary in war, and the sketchbook style makes that ordinariness the point. Tagline: degeneracy of modern warfare.

No. II, Bibi x NYC

A giant Benjamin Netanyahu, Israel's prime minister, in a suit and tie walks through a city that has been flattened, while tiny people run between his feet. The tagline, coming to a town near you, turns a far-away war into a question about your own street.

No. III, Ice x NYC

Masked men in body armour stand over someone lying on the ground, smoke behind them. No faces, no names. The tagline says it plainly: masked and unidentifiable. The drawing is about who gets to act without being seen.

No. IV, Niger Delta Oil Pirates

Masked gunmen ride a boat across open water. In 2011 a UN Environment Programme assessment found oil contamination in Ogoniland, in the Niger Delta, widespread and severely affecting the environment (UNEP). Tagline: degradation demanded dissidents.

Wearing it

The front of each tee is just a small IMPACT script, so the meaning only shows when you turn around. Unisex softstyle, S to L, $28.99, printed to order.

Why the meaning is not printed on the shirt

The taglines live on the site, not on the fabric. On the tee there is only the drawing. That is deliberate: the moment a shirt explains itself, people stop looking. Leave the picture alone and it keeps working every time someone new sees it.
