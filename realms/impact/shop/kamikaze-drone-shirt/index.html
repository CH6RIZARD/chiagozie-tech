Beware Killer Drones, the kamikaze drone shirt

"Kamikaze drone" went from a military term to something people type into search bars. The idea is simple and ugly: a drone that flies into its target and explodes. Beware Killer Drones, No. I in Impact Threads' Drop V·I, is a tee about that.

The print

On the back, a pencil-style drawing that looks like it came out of an old children's book. A few kids stand in a field, pointing up. One of them holds a string, and on the end of it, where the kite should be, is a drone. A farmhouse sits in the distance. "B.W.D." is lettered across the sky and "beware killer drones" is handwritten underneath. On the front, a small IMPACT script on the chest.

The record

In October 2022 Ukraine said Russia was striking towns deep inside its territory, including Bila Tserkva south of Kyiv, with what it described as Iranian-made Shahed-136 "kamikaze" drones. NBC News, which reported it, noted it could not independently verify the type of drone in the images Ukraine released (NBC News). Since then, drones have become a regular part of war reporting.

Why kids and a kite

The tagline is degeneracy of modern warfare. A weapon that kills from far away becomes ordinary enough that a generation grows up under it. The drawing puts that next to the most innocent picture there is, children flying a kite, and lets the gap do the talking. The shirt names no country and no people. It is aimed at a way of fighting and at the governments that choose it.

An anti drone shirt, not an anti tech shirt

If you searched for an anti drone shirt, this is the one, with a distinction: it is not against the camera drone your friend films skate clips with. It is against the ones built to kill.
