NYC graphic tees that are not souvenirs

Most NYC graphic tees are the same handful of things: the skyline, the subway map, a heart, a taxi. They are fine if you want to prove you visited. Impact Threads has two tees with NYC in the name, and neither one is a postcard.

Bibi x NYC

No. II in the drop. On the back, a full colour illustration of a giant Netanyahu in a suit and tie striding through a wrecked city while people run around his feet. On the front, the small IMPACT script sits on top of a thin city skyline, the only skyline you get. The tagline is coming to a town near you, and the NYC in the name is the point: it takes something happening far away and asks what it looks like on your block.

Ice x NYC

No. III. On the back, a sepia drawing of masked agents in body armour, rifles slung, standing over a person on the ground, with smoke behind them. On the front, just the IMPACT script. The tagline: masked and unidentifiable. The NYC here is about raids that happen in the city's own neighbourhoods.

Why New York

New York is where news turns into street culture fastest: it ends up on walls, on stickers, on the back of a tee on the L train by the end of the week. These two prints are made for that city and the people who move like it, wherever they actually live.
